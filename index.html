Questions people ask us

Straight answers about codes, earnings and payouts. Anything missing? Ask in our community groups.

How do I earn?

Every advertiser gets a personal code. When a client buys from one of our business partners using your code, the business gives a special discount on that purchase, and the discount amount is what you earn.

For example, if a laptop costs KSh 50,000 and your code gives a 5% discount, you earn 5% of KSh 50,000, which is KSh 2,500.

Does it work for online and in-store purchases?

Yes. Your code works for online orders, including delivery where the business offers it, and for purchases made in person at the business.

How much can I earn?

It depends on the products, the discount each business offers for your code, and how many clients use it. We can't promise any amount, so earnings are never guaranteed. The full rules are in our Terms of Service.

How do I get paid?

Your earnings are sent to the M-Pesa number you give when you sign up. The earnings are sent monthly, you will receive the payment from us.

How do you know a client used my code?

The business confirms each sale made with your code, and we record it on your account.

What can I promote, and who provides the products?

The products come from our business partners, and our team posts every available item on the goods page. When you sign up, you tick the categories you want to promote, such as electronics, home accessories, office equipment, clothes and shoes. You can change them at any time, and we add new categories as more goods become available.

Do I have to sell, deliver or deal with customers?

No. The business handles the sale, delivery where it offers it, and in-store service. Your job is to share the products with your followers using your code.

Do I need a lot of followers?

You need an audience that trusts you on Facebook, WhatsApp, Twitter or Telegram.

Does it cost anything to join?

Yes there is a one time fee of KSh 500 to secure your unique code which will be used by your clients when making purchases. The code will be used also when receiving your payment.

Why do you ask for my county, nearest city and M-Pesa number?

Your M-Pesa number is where your earnings are sent. Your county and nearest city help us show you businesses and offers near you. Read our Privacy Policy to see how we protect your information.

Where do I get help?

Join our community on Telegram or WhatsApp. The groups guide advertisers on what to do, and every question gets asked and answered there.

Ready to start earning?

Create your account, choose your categories and get your personal code. Questions? Our community groups are there to help.
